# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

This is a made-up handbook for a fictional company, Northwind Labs. It is used only as demo data for this project.

## 1. Working Hours

### 1.1 Core hours
Everyone is expected to be online between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days. Outside core hours you can plan your day as you like, as long as your team knows when you are available.

### 1.2 Overtime
Overtime is not expected. If you work more than 10 hours in a day for a release or an incident, you can take the extra hours as time off within the next 30 days. Overtime is not paid in cash.

### 1.3 Public holidays
Northwind Labs follows 10 fixed public holidays and 2 floating holidays each year. Floating holidays can be used for any festival you celebrate. Tell your manager at least one week before using a floating holiday.

## 2. Leave Policy

### 2.1 Annual leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of annual leave per year. Leave is added at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Days above 10 are lost on 31 March.

### 2.2 Sick leave
You get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over. If you are sick for more than 3 days in a row, share a doctor's note with HR.

### 2.3 Parental leave
Birth parents get 26 weeks of paid leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents, get 6 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected date.

### 2.4 How to apply for leave
Apply for all leave in the PeopleDesk app. For annual leave longer than 5 days, apply at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager must approve leave within 3 working days, or it is approved automatically.

## 3. Remote Work

### 3.1 Work from anywhere
You can work from any city in India. Working from outside India is allowed for up to 30 days per year, and needs written approval from your manager and the HR team because of tax rules.

### 3.2 Home office allowance
New employees get a one-time home office allowance of 25,000 rupees for a chair, desk or monitor. After that, you get 1,500 rupees per month for internet. Keep the bills for 1 year in case of audit.

### 3.3 Coworking spaces
If you prefer to work from a coworking space, the company pays up to 6,000 rupees per month. Book through the Workspace portal so the bill goes to the company directly.

## 4. Expenses and Travel

### 4.1 What you can claim
You can claim costs for client travel, team offsites, work books and online courses approved by your manager. Meals during work travel are covered up to 1,200 rupees per day.

### 4.2 How to claim
Submit claims in the PeopleDesk app within 30 days of spending. Attach a clear photo of the bill. Claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

### 4.3 Travel booking
Book flights and hotels through the travel desk at least 7 days before travel. Economy class is used for all flights shorter than 6 hours. For longer flights, premium economy is allowed.

## 5. Learning and Growth

### 5.1 Learning budget
Each employee gets a learning budget of 50,000 rupees per year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.

### 5.2 Conference days
You can take up to 3 working days per year to attend conferences. These days do not count as annual leave.

### 5.3 Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Salary changes are decided only in the April review. Promotions can happen in either cycle.

## 6. Security and IT

### 6.1 Laptops
Every employee gets a company laptop on day one. Do not install software that is not on the approved list without IT approval. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within 24 hours.

### 6.2 Passwords and 2FA
Use the company password manager for all work accounts. Two-factor authentication is required for email, code hosting and cloud accounts.

### 6.3 Customer data
Never copy customer data to your personal devices or personal cloud storage. Customer data must stay in approved company systems. Breaking this rule can lead to termination.

## 7. Leaving the Company

### 7.1 Notice period
The notice period is 60 days for all employees. It can be shortened if your manager agrees and the handover is complete.

### 7.2 Final settlement
Your final salary, unused annual leave and pending claims are paid within 45 days of your last working day. Sick leave is not paid out.

### 7.3 Returning equipment
Return your laptop and any other company equipment on or before your last working day. The cost of missing equipment is taken from the final settlement.
