# Sunrise Family Clinic: FAQ

This is made-up demo data for a fictional clinic.

## timings
Q: What are the clinic timings?
A: We are open Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM. On Sunday we are open 9 AM to 1 PM.

## location
Q: Where is the clinic?
A: We are at 14 Lake View Road, Indiranagar, next to the City Bank ATM.

## parking
Q: Is there parking?
A: Yes. Free parking for 10 cars in the basement. Two-wheelers can park in front.

## book
Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: Call this number or book on our website. Walk-ins are welcome, but booked patients are seen first.

## cancel
Q: How do I cancel or move my appointment?
A: Call us at least 2 hours before your slot. There is no cancellation fee.

## fees
Q: What is the consultation fee?
A: A general consultation is 600 rupees. A follow-up visit within 7 days is free.

## insurance
Q: Do you accept insurance?
A: Yes. We offer cashless visits with Star Health, HDFC Ergo and Niva Bupa. Bring your insurance card and a photo ID.

## payment
Q: How can I pay?
A: We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards.

## reports
Q: When will my blood test report be ready?
A: Most blood test reports are ready in 24 hours. We send them on WhatsApp and email.

## fasting
Q: Do I need to fast before a blood test?
A: For sugar and cholesterol tests, do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before. You can drink water.

## home-collection
Q: Can you collect a blood sample from home?
A: Yes. Home sample collection is available from 7 AM to 11 AM for a fee of 200 rupees.

## doctors
Q: Which doctors are available and when?
A: Dr. Anjali Sharma, general physician, sees patients every day. Dr. Rohan Mehta, child specialist, is in on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings.

## child-vaccination
Q: Do you give vaccines to children?
A: Yes. We follow the national vaccination schedule. Please bring your child's vaccination card.

## teleconsult
Q: Can I talk to a doctor on video?
A: Yes. Video consultations are available from 6 PM to 8 PM on weekdays. The fee is 400 rupees.

## languages
Q: Which languages do your staff speak?
A: Our staff speak English, Hindi and Kannada.

## prescription-refill
Q: Can I get my regular medicine prescription renewed?
A: Yes, but the doctor must see you, in person or on video, at least once every 3 months.

## records
Q: How do I get a copy of my medical records?
A: Ask at the front desk or email records@sunriseclinic.example. We share them within 2 working days.

## wheelchair
Q: Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
A: Yes. There is a ramp at the entrance and a lift to the first floor.
