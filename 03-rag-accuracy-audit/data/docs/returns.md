# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original box.

## How to start a return
Go to My Orders, pick the item and click Start a return. Print the free return label and drop the parcel at any partner courier point.

## When you get your money back
We send the refund to your original payment method within 5 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives broken or is not what you ordered, send a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We send a free replacement and you do not need to return the broken item.

## Items you cannot return
Mattresses, pillows and cut-to-size curtains cannot be returned once opened, for hygiene and custom-made reasons.
