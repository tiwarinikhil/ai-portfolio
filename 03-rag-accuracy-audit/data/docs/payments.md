# Payments

## Ways to pay
We accept UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery limit
Cash on delivery is available only for orders below 10,000 rupees.

## No-cost EMI
Orders above 5,000 rupees can be split into 3 or 6 monthly instalments with no interest on selected credit cards.

## Payment failed but money was taken
If money left your account but the order was not placed, the bank reverses it automatically within 7 days. You do not need to contact us.

## GST invoice
Download a GST invoice from My Orders. To add your company GSTIN, enter it at checkout before you pay.
