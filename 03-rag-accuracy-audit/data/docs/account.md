# Your account

## Reset your password
Click Forgot password on the sign-in page. We email a reset link that works for 30 minutes.

## Change your delivery address
You can change the address on an order until it is packed. After that, the address is locked.

## Delete your account
Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Delete account. We remove your personal data within 30 days, but keep invoices for 8 years as the law requires.

## Brightleaf Club points
You earn 1 point for every 100 rupees you spend. 100 points equal a 100 rupee discount on your next order.

Need more help? Our support team can help with orders, returns, refunds, shipping, delivery, payments, warranty claims and account questions. Write to help@brightleaf.example and we reply within one business day. Brightleaf Home is a made-up demo store.
