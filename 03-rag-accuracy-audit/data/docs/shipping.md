# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and up to 8 business days elsewhere.

## Express delivery
Express delivery arrives in 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat 199 rupees per order.

## Free shipping
Orders above 2,999 rupees ship free with standard delivery.

## Large furniture
Sofas, beds and wardrobes come by our own van team. They call you a day before to book a two-hour slot and carry the item to your room.

## Tracking your parcel
You get an SMS with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Missed delivery
The courier tries three times. After the third missed attempt, the parcel comes back to us and we refund you minus the shipping fee.
