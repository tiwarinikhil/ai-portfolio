# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original box.

## How to start a return
Email us your order number and we will send a return label within two days. Drop the parcel at any partner courier point.

## When you get your money back
We send the refund to your original payment method within 10 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives broken or is not what you ordered, send a photo within 24 hours of delivery. We send a replacement after the broken item is collected.

Need more help? Our support team can help with orders, returns, refunds, shipping, delivery, payments, warranty claims and account questions. Write to help@brightleaf.example and we reply within one business day. Brightleaf Home is a made-up demo store.
