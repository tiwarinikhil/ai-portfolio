# Warranty

## Furniture warranty
Wooden furniture has a 2-year warranty against cracks, loose joints and manufacturing faults.

## Lighting warranty
Lamps and LED lights have a 1-year warranty on the electrical parts.

## What the warranty does not cover
Normal wear, water damage, sun fading and damage from moving the item yourself are not covered.

## How to make a claim
Fill in the claim form on the Warranty page with your order number and photos. A technician visits within 7 days to repair or replace the item.
